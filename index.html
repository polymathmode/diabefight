Warning Signs You May Be At Risk

Diabetes can develop quietly. If you notice several of these signs, consider checking your blood sugar and speaking to a health professional.

Common signs

  • Frequent urination (especially at night)
  • Excessive thirst or dry mouth
  • Unusual fatigue or low energy
  • Blurred vision
  • Slow healing of cuts or wounds
  • Tingling/numbness in hands or feet

What can happen over time

  • Reduced circulation and nerve health
  • Higher risk of infections
  • Difficulty maintaining stable blood sugar after meals
  • Overall reduced quality of life
